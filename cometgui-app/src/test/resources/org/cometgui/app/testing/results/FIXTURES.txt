Percolator wrote real/percolator-3.07.1/peptides.tsv byte-identical to its
psms.tsv, and decoy-peptides.tsv to decoy-psms.tsv (64 + 64 synthetic PIN), so
ResultsCountsUiTest uses each copy for both tables. Its counts are awk-pinned
in that test's documentation, with the command.

The GUI tests put psms-unknown-q.tsv in a constructed run as its target PSM
table (15 rows; q-values 0.01 and 0.010 exactly at the default cutoff,
0.0100001 just above it, and every unknown kind) and psms-shuffled.tsv as its
target peptide table (23 rows; the two tables share a header, as Percolator's
do). The expected counts each test asserts are worked out by hand from these
files, row by row, in the test's own documentation.

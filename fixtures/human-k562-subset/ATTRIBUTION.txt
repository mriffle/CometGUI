uniprot-human-2026_02-k562-subset.fasta -- attribution and provenance
======================================================================

What this file is
-----------------
A SUBSET of the UniProt reference proteome of Homo sapiens (human): proteome
UP000005640, NCBI taxonomy identifier 9606, canonical sequences. It is NOT
the proteome: it holds 3,698 of its 20,652 records. The records it holds are
UniProt's own, each copied byte for byte and in the order of UniProt's file.

It is the protein database of CometGUI's test fixture, cut to suit the two
K562 spectrum files that fixture searches. Do not use it for anything else: a
search of other spectra against it would be a search against a database
chosen to contain these spectra's answers.

Changes made: a subset of 3,698 of 20,652 records, selected by the two rules
below, otherwise unaltered. The source was decompressed with gzip; no record
was edited, reordered or added, and every kept record is the source's bytes.

  * 698 records are the proteins behind the confident identifications of the
    two K562 spectrum files: every protein Percolator names for a target PSM
    or a target peptide with a q-value of 0.01 or less, in a search of both
    files against the whole source by any Comet and Percolator pair CometGUI
    offers on Linux x86-64 (Comet 2026.02.2 and 2026.03.0, Percolator 3.06.5
    and 3.07.1), with Comet reporting five results per spectrum and one.
  * 3,000 records are background: the other records of the source ranked by
    the SHA-256 of their accession, the first 3,000 taken.

accessions.tsv, beside this file, lists every kept accession and which of the
two it was kept as. scripts/fixtures/fixture_fasta.py holds both rules and
rebuilds this file from the source and that list, byte for byte:

    bash scripts/fixtures/fixture-fasta.sh verify --source UP000005640_9606.fasta

docs/developer/test_fixtures.rst records why 3,000, with the measurements.

Why it is here
--------------
It is the protein database of CometGUI's test fixture (DECISIONS.rst, D-006
and D-018): the two K562 mzML files, 20100614_Velos1_TaGe_SA_K562_3.mzML and
_4.mzML of crux-toolkit's smoke tests, searched against this file. The
spectrum files are fetched by checksum and are not in this repository.
